Is it the issuer's token?

Coinbase lists its stock tokens on Base and Robinhood lists its own on Robinhood Chain. Only an exact contract address from those lists counts. A token that copies the name or symbol is marked not official, and you get a warning before any link to it opens.

02

What does the issuer say it's worth?

The issuer's price for one token (Coinbase NAV or Robinhood token bid/ask), always with its age. These prices stop moving while the US market is closed. A price from 40 hours ago is normal on a Sunday and odd on a Tuesday afternoon.

reference $188.42 · 41 h ago · market closed
03

How far has the chain drifted?

Every 15 minutes we compare the deepest on-chain pool with that price and keep the history, split into market open, extended hours and closed. Under 0.5% stays grey. Amber is a gap, red is a big gap.

+0.13% +1.40% +4.12%

Where the gaps open

From Friday close to Monday open the issuer's price stays the same for 65 hours, but the tokens keep trading. Holidays make that longer. This is when the on-chain price can move away from the share.

Each passport shows the last 72 hours with closed hours shaded.

Ask it from your agent

The same passport works as a public MCP server. Anyone can connect an agent to it without a key or an account. It has six read-only tools: find_stock_token, list_stock_tokens, stock_passport, verify_stock_tokens (up to 50 addresses before a buy), recent_impostors and compare_venues. They return data with its time and source. There is no tool that trades. Tool reference

Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector → paste the URL. Claude Code: run the command. Other MCP clients: the JSON.
